    Load your Google Photos

    Google no longer lets apps read your photo library or its locations through the API, so this map reads a Google Takeout export instead. Everything stays in this browser. Nothing is uploaded.

    1. Go to takeout.google.com, click Deselect all, then check Google Photos.
    2. Choose .zip as the file type. Any size per file works.
    3. When Google emails you, download every part and drop them all here (or unzip and choose the folder).
    Drop Takeout .zip files or a Takeout folder here
    Only the small .json sidecar files are read. Photos themselves are never opened.
